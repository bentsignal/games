Privacy

This is a private game for friends, hosted at ticket.bentsignal.com.

Account and game data

Google sign-in identifies your account. Google provides basic profile information during sign-in; we do not request access to Gmail, Drive, contacts, or other Google content. We store an account identifier, your chosen username, game participation, results, and chat messages.

Your username, moves, and chat are visible to people in your game. Destination tickets and cards stay private during play, subject to the game rules.

Storage and services

Convex stores accounts, authentication sessions, and game data. Vercel hosts the website. Your browser stores sign-in tokens and game preferences so you can return to your games. Optional music uses an embedded YouTube player, which connects directly to YouTube.

Games and chat are retained so players can resume games and retain their history. Completed results may be used for personal game statistics. This game does not sell personal data or include advertising analytics.

Your choices

You can sign out at any time. Contact the friend who hosts this game to request deletion of your account or game data. You can also revoke the app’s Google access in your Google Account settings.
